Meet the gardener

Hi, I'm Harlan.

Hi I'm Harlan. You might have heard my name from the rest of this website. This is my little gardening and landscaping business.

From full backyard overhauls to regular garden maintenance, I'm here to help your garden dream become your daily joy.

Harlan's Gardens is about growth and care. My hands-on approach makes it easy to fill a garden bed full of your favorite flowers, cut your lawn just right, or turn hard clay dirt into softer nutrient-rich soil. So if you're a new gardener who wants a little horticulture therapy or a permaculture enthusiast who needs an extra hand with your compost plan, I'm here to help.

So let's talk about how you want to grow your backyard and cultivate a space you love.
